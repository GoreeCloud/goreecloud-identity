# GoreeCloud Identity — Competitive Objectives

Capability references include mature self-hosted identity providers and major platform account systems, but GoreeCloud Identity must remain an original GoreeCloud-owned platform implementation.

Objectives:

- Provide modern account, OIDC/OAuth, device, passkey, session, recovery, and delegated-authority experiences appropriate for GoreeCloud applications.
- Keep human and workload identity policy under a coherent first-party authority.
- Make privacy-preserving consumer discovery a default design property rather than exposing a broadly enumerable directory.
- Provide clear security, recovery, auditing, and administrative evidence through Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, and Everkeep integrations.
- Coordinate service identity and capability authorization through GoreeCloud Mesh without giving applications arbitrary credential-issuance authority.
- Deliver accessible, responsive Identity Center experiences using Glaze UI 2.0 or newer.
- Preserve portability and migration paths without making any upstream provider architecture permanent.

Competitive parity is an objective, not a present implementation claim. Stable qualification requires native implementation and acceptance evidence.
