# GoreeCloud Identity — Benefits

GoreeCloud Identity is intended to give GoreeCloud one accountable authority for human and workload identity without making each application invent its own account, credential, session, or recovery rules.

Primary intended benefits:

- Consistent user/session/device authority across GoreeCloud applications.
- Narrow service credentials with centralized verification and rotation policy.
- Privacy-minimized consumer discovery rather than an enumerable default directory.
- Shared recovery, delegated authority, and audit semantics across the ecosystem.
- Reduced credential duplication and application-specific authentication drift.
- First-party integration with Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, Everkeep, GoreeCloud Mesh, and Glaze UI.
- A native long-term architecture that does not depend on preserving an upstream provider implementation.

These are product objectives unless a capability is explicitly identified as implemented and validated.
