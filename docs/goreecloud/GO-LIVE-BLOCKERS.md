# GoreeCloud Identity Go-Live Blockers

The following items remain release-blocking until validated on the exact candidate and target environment:

- Exact-head inherited and GoreeCloud CI completion.
- Manual Glaze UI 1.3 and accessibility acceptance across representative security-critical surfaces.
- Approved canonical GoreeCloud Identity application artwork.
- Private DNS, NetBird, Caddy, HTTPS, firewall, and backend publication validation.
- Independent availability monitoring and alerting.
- At least one real GoreeCloud OIDC relying-application integration with success, denial, failure, logout, recovery, and rollback evidence.
- Database/configuration backup and isolated restoration.
- Recovery of signing/encryption material required for continuity.
- Tested break-glass administration.
- Upgrade and rollback validation.
- Production cutover approval and post-cutover monitoring criteria.

No source merge alone clears these blockers.
