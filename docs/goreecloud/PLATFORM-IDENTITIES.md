# GoreeCloud Identity Platform Identity Boundaries

GoreeCloud Identity uses GoreeCloud platform identities according to their distinct roles.

- **Glaze UI** is the application-wide design and interaction language.
- **Wardveil Security by GoreeCloud** is the security and protection identity for evidence-backed security controls and status.
- **GoreeCloud Privacy Shield** is the privacy identity where privacy protections are explicitly surfaced.
- **Everkeep** is the resilience, backup, recovery, preservation, and continuity identity where those capabilities are surfaced.

These identities do not replace the technical systems underneath them. Product presentation must be backed by actual security, privacy, recovery, accessibility, or operational evidence as applicable.
