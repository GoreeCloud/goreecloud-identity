# GoreeCloud Identity Final Product Boundary

The intended final product is a cohesive GoreeCloud-controlled identity experience built on the mature authentik engine, not a loose collection of theme overrides.

The finished product must present GoreeCloud Identity consistently across authentication, enrollment, recovery, account security, user, administrator, error, loading, notification, and system-generated experiences; preserve upstream protocol/security correctness; integrate through documented standards-based interfaces; provide evidence-backed Wardveil Security presentation; minimize privacy exposure; remain recoverable; and be operable through documented monitoring, logging, upgrade, and rollback paths.

Until those requirements are demonstrated on an exact release candidate in the target environment, the product remains in development.
