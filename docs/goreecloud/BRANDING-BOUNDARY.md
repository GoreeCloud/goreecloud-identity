# GoreeCloud Identity Branding Boundary

GoreeCloud Identity is the product name for the maintained authentik fork. Product-facing presentation should use GoreeCloud Identity and Glaze UI where legally and technically permitted while preserving required upstream licensing, attribution, provenance, and package identities.

A unique canonical GoreeCloud Identity application mark remains required before Stable acceptance. Branding must not alter or obscure security-critical labels, consent meaning, recovery warnings, authenticator state, administrative consequences, or legal attribution.
